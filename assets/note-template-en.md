## Critical Evaluation

**Q1: What the paper actually says vs. what the authors claim it says**
(Compare methods and results to the abstract/discussion; identify overclaims)

**Q2: What does this study NOT measure?**
(What unstated assumptions are embedded in the method? Would measuring X substantially weaken the core conclusion?)

**Q3: How far does the conclusion generalize?**
(If replicated in a different population/context/time, how would the results most likely change? What are the boundary conditions of this theory?)

**Q4: Which important citations are missing?**
(What foundational literature should this paper have engaged with but did not?)

**Q5: What is the boundary condition of the theory?**
